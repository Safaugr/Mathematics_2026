# Exercise 9. A System from a Sum Condition

## Question

Find numbers x and y satisfying

$$
x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.
$$

Solve the system and explain geometrically
why the solution is unique.

## Solution

### 1. Eliminate x

Subtract twice the first equation from the second:

$$
(2x+5y)-2(x+y)=32-2\cdot10.
$$

Simplifying gives

$$
3y=12,
\qquad
y=4.
$$

Substitute into the first equation:

$$
x+4=10,
\qquad
x=6.
$$

Therefore,

$$
(x,y)=(6,4).
$$

### 2. Geometric explanation of uniqueness

Write the equations in slope-intercept form:

$$
y=10-x,
$$

$$
y=\frac{32}{5}-\frac25x.
$$

The slopes are -1 and -2/5, which are different.
Therefore, the lines intersect at exactly one point.

Their intersection is (6,4), so the system
has exactly one solution.

### 3. Verification

Substitute into the original equations:

$$
6+4=10,
$$

$$
2\cdot6+5\cdot4=12+20=32.
$$

Both conditions are satisfied.
